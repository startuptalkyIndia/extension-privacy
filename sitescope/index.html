SiteScope — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-19 (version 0.3.0)

SiteScope is a Chrome extension that shows what the web page in your current tab is made of: technologies, fonts, colours, SEO details, headings and links.

What SiteScope reads

Only when you click the SiteScope icon, it reads the page in that one tab: the page's markup, its computed styles, the addresses of its scripts, stylesheets and links, its meta tags, the page's own host name, the names of some public page variables, and the NAMES of cookies that scripts on the page can see (never their values; used only to recognise technologies such as a framework's session cookie). Nothing read this way is stored or sent anywhere. It uses Chrome's activeTab permission, so it has no access to any other tab and no standing access to any website.

What is stored, and where

The report itself is built in memory and shown in the popup; when the popup closes it is gone. SiteScope does not save your browsing history or the pages you analyse. The only things kept, in your browser's local extension storage on your device, are three small counters for the rating reminder described below: the install date, how many times you have run a scan, and whether the reminder was dismissed.

What is sent over the network

Nothing. SiteScope makes no network requests, has no server, no account system, no analytics and no advertising. We do not receive any of your data.

Uninstall page

When you remove SiteScope, Chrome opens a page on our policy site (startuptalkyindia.github.io). The address contains only the extension name, its version and its ID. Nothing else is sent from the page; it shows an optional "what went wrong?" form that only opens an email draft you can choose to send. The website host may keep ordinary access logs, as any website does.

Rating reminder

After at least 7 days and 10 scans, the popup shows one small card asking if you would like to rate SiteScope on the Chrome Web Store. It is shown the same way to everyone, offers no reward, appears only inside the SiteScope popup (never on websites), and is shown at most twice: once, then once more 30 days after "Not now". "Don't ask again" ends it. It uses only the local counters above, which are never sent anywhere.

Copy and download

"Copy report" puts the report on your clipboard as text when you press the button. "JSON" saves the report as a file in your Downloads folder when you press the button. These stay on your device. What you do with them afterwards is up to you.

What we do not do

We do not sell or share data, use it for advertising, use it to judge creditworthiness, or transfer it to anyone. SiteScope does not load or run remote code.

Permissions

activeTab (read the tab you click the icon on), scripting (run SiteScope's own packaged analysis in that tab) and storage (the local counters for the rating reminder). No host permissions.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the new text is published at the same address.
