PDF Toolbox - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-26 (version 0.1.1)

PDF Toolbox is a Chrome extension that merges, splits, organises, compresses, signs and converts PDF files on your own computer. It opens its tool page in a browser tab when you click its toolbar icon. It has no server and no account system.

Contact: extensionsforseo@gmail.com

What PDF Toolbox reads

Only the files you choose: PDFs and pictures that you pick with the file chooser or drop onto the page. It cannot see other files, other tabs, websites or your browsing history, and it requests no access to any website.

What is collected and sent

Nothing is collected and nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. Your files are read and processed inside the browser tab on your device (in memory, partly in a background worker of the extension). PDF Toolbox makes no network requests of its own (it only loads its own bundled files, such as fonts and decoders, from inside the extension): it has no analytics, no advertising and no remote code. All its code and the two libraries it uses (pdf-lib and PDF.js, with their fonts and decoders) are bundled in the extension.

Results are saved with an ordinary browser download from the page. The browser then treats them like any other downloaded file.

What is stored, where and for how long

  • Preferences: the resolution and quality last used for Strong compression, the page size, margin and orientation for pictures to PDF, and the picture format and resolution for PDF to pictures. Kept in your browser (chrome.storage.local) until you change them or remove the extension.

  • Reminder counters: the install date, the number of results you saved (a number only), and the state of the optional rating reminder (not shown, shown, snoozed, finished; how many times shown; when it was snoozed). Kept in your browser (chrome.storage.local), never sent anywhere, until you remove the extension.

  • In memory only, while the tool page is open: the files you chose and the results being built, a signature you draw or type (and its picture), and any text you place. All of it is gone when you close or reload the tab. Nothing about your files, their names or their contents is written to browser storage or to any server.

  • A password you type for a protected PDF is held in memory only while that file is open in the tab (PDF.js needs it to read the pages), and is gone when you choose another file, close the file or close the tab. The extension does not keep a copy of its own, never writes it to storage and never sends it anywhere. The password box asks the browser not to offer to save it.

  • Pictures you add in Pictures to PDF are shown as small previews drawn in the page; no file address is kept for them.

Uninstall page

When you remove PDF Toolbox, Chrome opens a page on our policy site (startuptalkyindia.github.io). The address contains only the extension name, its version and its ID. Nothing is sent from that page; it offers a form that opens an email draft which you may choose to send. The site host may keep ordinary access logs.

Rating reminder

PDF Toolbox keeps local counters (install date, number of results saved, reminder state) in your browser. After at least 7 days and 10 saved results it may show one small card on its own tool page asking you to rate it. It is shown at most twice, only on that page (never on a website, and never as a notification or a new tab), is the same for everyone, and the counters are never sent anywhere. Choosing "Rate" or "Send feedback" opens the Chrome Web Store reviews page or an email draft only because you clicked.

What we do not do

We do not sell or share your data, use it for advertising or credit decisions, or transfer it to anyone. PDF Toolbox does not upload your files, does not load or run remote code, and does not read any website.

Permissions

  • storage: save your preferences and the local reminder counters. No other permission is requested: no website access, no downloads permission, no clipboard access.

Limits worth knowing

A drawn or typed signature placed with PDF Toolbox is a picture; it is not a cryptographic or legally certified digital signature. Strong compression turns pages into pictures. Unlocking works only with the correct password and produces a copy made of page pictures. Files that only restrict editing or copying need no password; the editing tools use a copy made of page pictures for those too. Saving a new file with any tool (including Sign and Light compression) invalidates any digital signature the original had, and merge, split, organise, page numbers, watermark and clean-up do not keep bookmarks, jump-to-page links or interactive form fields. Remove metadata deletes the document information, the XMP packets (document and page level) and private application data, and leaves no copy of them in the file; metadata inside pictures, fonts or attachments is not touched. Adding a password to a PDF is not supported.
