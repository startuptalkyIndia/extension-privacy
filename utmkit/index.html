UTM Link Builder — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-07 (version 0.1.0)

UTM Link Builder is a Chrome extension that helps you add campaign tags (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign and similar) to a web address and copy the result.

What UTM Link Builder reads

When you open the toolbar popup, it reads the address of the tab you are on to fill the page-address box (and again if you press "This tab"). You can change or clear that address. It does not read the content of pages, forms, passwords, cookies or anything you type on websites, and it has no script that runs on websites.

What is stored, and where

Only the following, saved in your browser (chrome.storage.local) on your device:

  • your saved presets (the tags only, never a page address);

  • the last 50 links you copied (the full link, so you can copy it again);

  • the last tag values, mode and channels you used, so the popup reopens where you left off (never the page address);

  • your three naming options (lowercase, how spaces are written, replace existing tags);

  • three counters for one rating reminder: when it was installed, how many times you copied a link, and how many times the reminder was shown and when, and whether you dismissed it for good. Removing the extension deletes all of this. You can clear recent links from the popup at any time.

What is sent over the network

Nothing. UTM Link Builder makes no network requests. It has no server, no account system, no analytics and no advertising. We do not receive any of your data.

Uninstall page

When you remove UTM Link Builder, Chrome opens a page on our policy site (startuptalkyindia.github.io). The address contains only the extension name, its version and its ID. The page sends nothing; it shows a short "what went wrong?" form that opens an email draft you may choose to send. The site host may keep ordinary access logs.

Rating reminder

After at least 7 days and 10 copied links, the toolbar popup (never a website) shows one small card asking you to rate the extension. It is shown at most twice, can be dismissed for good, offers no reward, and the counters are never sent anywhere.

What we do not do

We do not collect, sell or share your data, use it for advertising, use it to judge creditworthiness, or transfer it to anyone. UTM Link Builder does not load or run remote code.

Permissions

  • activeTab: so the popup can read the address of the tab you opened it on, to fill the address box.

  • storage: to save your presets, recent links, last values, options and the reminder counters on your device. It asks for no access to websites in advance, no tabs permission, and has no always-on script.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the new text is published at the same address.
