Zonely - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-19

Zonely is a browser extension that lets you choose the time zone that web pages see.

What Zonely reads

Zonely runs on web pages (http and https) and replaces the page's date and time-zone functions so they report the zone you chose. It does not read the text of pages, forms, passwords, cookies, links or anything you type. When you open the toolbar popup it reads the address of the current tab only to show and edit the rule for that site. When you press "Check the current tab" it reads the time-zone values the page reports and shows them to you; they are not stored or sent anywhere.

What is stored, and where

Only your own settings, saved in your browser (chrome.storage.local) on your device: the zone you chose, the sites you switched on or off, per-site zones, recently used zones, the optional switch-back time, the zones you compare, and whether the tab reloads automatically. Removing the extension deletes them. The backup text you can export is created on your screen only; Zonely does not send it anywhere.

What is sent over the network

Nothing. Zonely makes no network requests. It has no server, no account system, no analytics and no advertising. We do not receive any of your data.

What we do not do

We do not collect, sell or share your data, use it for advertising, use it to judge creditworthiness, or transfer it to anyone. Zonely does not load or run remote code.

Uninstall page

When you remove Zonely, Chrome opens a page on our policy site (startuptalkyindia.github.io). The address contains only the extension name, its version and its ID. Nothing is sent from that page by Zonely; it offers a form that opens an email draft which you may choose to send. The site's host may keep ordinary access logs.

Rating reminder

After at least 7 days, and after you have chosen a time zone at least 10 times, the popup shows one small card asking you to consider rating Zonely. It is shown to everyone equally, offers nothing in return, and appears at most twice (the second time only 30 or more days after the first). Rating, sending feedback or choosing "Don't ask again" ends it for good. It counts these things locally in your browser (install date, number of zone choices, your choice on the card) and never sends them anywhere. It appears only inside the popup, never on websites.

What Zonely does not change

Zonely does not change your computer's clock and does not hide your IP address. It is not an anonymity tool.

Permissions

  • storage: to save your settings.

  • scripting: to apply the chosen zone to pages before they run, and to run the "Check the current tab" button.

  • alarms: for the optional "switch back after" timer.

  • Access to http and https pages: so the chosen zone can be applied to the sites you use.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the new text is published at the same address.
