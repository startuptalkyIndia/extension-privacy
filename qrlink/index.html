QR Link — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-07 (version 0.1.0)

QR Link is a Chrome extension that makes QR codes from a web address, text, Wi-Fi details, an email or a phone number that you enter, and lets you download or copy the picture.

What QR Link reads

When you open the toolbar popup, it reads the address of the tab you are on to fill the address box (and again if you press "This tab"). You can change or clear it. It also uses whatever you type into its own fields. It does not read the content of pages, forms, passwords, cookies or anything on websites, and it has no script that runs on websites.

What is stored, and where

Only the following, saved in your browser (chrome.storage.local) on your device:

  • your five choices: the type of code you last used, the error-correction level, the download size, and the two colours;

  • three counters for one rating reminder: when it was installed, how many times you downloaded or copied a code, and how many times the reminder was shown and when, and whether you dismissed it for good. What you enter to make a code (a link, text, a Wi-Fi name and password, an email address, a phone number) is never saved. Removing the extension deletes everything above.

What is sent over the network

Nothing. QR Link makes no network requests. The code is drawn on your computer by software bundled inside the extension. It has no server, no account system, no analytics and no advertising. We do not receive your links, passwords or any other data.

Files and the clipboard

When you press Download, the picture is made on your computer and saved by your browser like any download. When you press Copy image, the picture is placed on your clipboard. Neither happens unless you press the button.

Uninstall page

When you remove QR Link, Chrome opens a page on our policy site (startuptalkyindia.github.io). The address contains only the extension name, its version and its ID. The page sends nothing; it shows a short "what went wrong?" form that opens an email draft you may choose to send. The site host may keep ordinary access logs.

Rating reminder

After at least 7 days and 10 downloads or copies, the toolbar popup (never a website) shows one small card asking you to rate the extension. It is shown at most twice, can be dismissed for good, offers no reward, and the counters are never sent anywhere.

Third-party software

QR Link bundles the open-source "QR Code Generator for JavaScript" by Kazuhiko Arase (MIT licence). It runs inside the extension and makes no network requests. "QR Code" is a registered trademark of DENSO WAVE INCORPORATED.

What we do not do

We do not collect, sell or share your data, use it for advertising, use it to judge creditworthiness, or transfer it to anyone. QR Link does not load or run remote code.

Permissions

  • activeTab: so the popup can read the address of the tab you opened it on, to fill the address box.

  • storage: to save your five choices and the reminder counters on your device. It asks for no access to websites in advance, no tabs permission, and has no always-on script.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the new text is published at the same address.
