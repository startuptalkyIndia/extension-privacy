OneQuote privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-19 (version 0.2.2)

OneQuote shows one quote a day. It is built to keep your data on your own device.

What OneQuote stores (on your device only)

  • The quotes you mark as favourites.

  • Your chosen theme filter and your chosen card colour theme.

  • The date it was installed, a count of how many times you opened it, and whether you dismissed the rating reminder.

  • The dates (not times) on which you opened OneQuote, kept for about the last 400 days, so it can show a day-streak line.

This is saved with the browser's extension storage (chrome.storage.local). It is never sent to the developer or to anyone else. Removing the extension deletes it.

What OneQuote does not do

  • It makes no network requests of its own (the only web address involved is the uninstall page described below, opened by Chrome when you remove the extension). It works fully offline.

  • It does not read the pages you visit, your browsing history, your tabs or anything else in your browser.

  • It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not share or sell data.

Uninstall page When you remove OneQuote, Chrome opens a page on our policy site (startuptalkyindia.github.io). The address contains only the extension name, its version and its extension ID. Nothing is sent from that page by OneQuote or by the page itself; its optional feedback form only opens an email draft that you may choose to send. The website host may keep ordinary access logs.

Rating reminder After you have had OneQuote for at least 7 days and opened it at least 10 times, the popup may show one small card asking for a rating. It is shown the same way to everyone, at most twice (the second time only after 30 days, if you chose "Not now"), and never again after "Don't ask again". It uses only local counters (install date, number of opens, reminder state), which are never sent anywhere.

Quote cards When you press "Download PNG", the image is created on your computer and saved through your browser's normal download. It is not uploaded anywhere.

Quotes The bundled quotes come from public-domain works and short, well-known sayings.
