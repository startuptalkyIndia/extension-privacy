App Finder for Wix — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-11 (version 0.1.0)

App Finder for Wix is a Chrome extension that shows which apps a Wix site lists in its own page data, for the page you have open. It is not made by or connected to Wix.

What App Finder for Wix reads

Only when you open the extension's popup while a web page is open, it reads the data that page already contains about the apps on the site (app names, app IDs and the addresses the apps load from). It reads that one page only, never other tabs or other pages, and it does not read forms, passwords, cookies or what you type on the page. It runs nothing on any page until you open the popup, and it has no script that runs on websites by itself.

What is stored, and where

Only the following, saved in your browser (chrome.storage.local) on your device:

  • if you press "Save site": the site's host name (for example example.com, never the full address), the date, and its app list (names, IDs, widget counts);

  • your last filter text and the tab you were on;

  • three counters for one rating reminder: when it was installed, how many times you used it, and how many times the reminder was shown and when, and whether you dismissed it for good. Removing the extension deletes all of this. "Remove" and "Remove all" delete saved sites at any time.

What is sent over the network

Nothing. App Finder for Wix makes no network requests. It has no server, no account system, no analytics and no advertising. We do not receive the pages you open, the app lists or any other data. A CSV or copied list leaves the extension only when you choose to download or paste it.

Uninstall page

When you remove App Finder for Wix, Chrome opens a page on our policy site (startuptalkyindia.github.io). The address contains only the extension name, its version and its ID. The page sends nothing; it shows a short "what went wrong?" form that opens an email draft you may choose to send. The site host may keep ordinary access logs.

Rating reminder

After at least 7 days and 10 uses, the toolbar popup (never a website) shows one small card asking you to rate the extension. It is shown at most twice, can be dismissed for good, offers no reward, and the counters are never sent anywhere.

What we do not do

We do not collect, sell or share your data, use it for advertising, use it to judge creditworthiness, or transfer it to anyone. App Finder for Wix does not load or run remote code. Wix is a trademark of its owner; this extension is independent.

Permissions

  • activeTab: lets the extension read the page you are looking at, only when you open the popup. No host permissions are requested.

  • scripting: runs the extension's own bundled reader on that one tab. No remote code.

  • storage: saves your saved sites, your filter and the reminder counters on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the new text is published at the same address.
