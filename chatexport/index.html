ChatExport - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-24 (version 0.1.2)

ChatExport is a Chrome extension that saves the AI conversation you have open on chatgpt.com or claude.ai as a file (PDF, Word, Markdown, HTML, text, CSV or JSON).

Contact: extensionsforseo@gmail.com

What ChatExport reads

Only when you open the ChatExport popup on a tab, it reads the conversation shown on that tab (the messages, the title and the page address) so it can build your file. It does not read other tabs, your browsing history, or anything on other websites, and it has no always-on scripts.

What is collected and sent

Nothing is collected and nothing is sent. ChatExport has no server, no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and makes no network requests. The conversation text is processed only inside your browser at the moment you export, then the file is created on your own device. We never receive your conversations.

What is stored, and where

  • Settings (file name template, light/dark choice, page size, option switches) are kept in your browser (chrome.storage.local) on your device.

  • Library (optional): only if you press "Save to library", the chosen messages are stored in your browser's own database (IndexedDB) on your device so you can search, merge and export them later. If "Hide sensitive data" is on at that moment, the saved copy has that data replaced. You can delete saved chats at any time from the library page, and removing the extension removes them.

  • The text of a conversation is otherwise held in memory only until the file is written.

Uninstall page

When you remove ChatExport, Chrome opens a page on our policy site (startuptalkyindia.github.io). The address contains only the extension name, its version and its ID. Nothing is sent from that page; it offers a form that opens an email draft which you may choose to send. The site host may keep ordinary access logs.

Rating reminder

ChatExport keeps local counters (install date, number of exports, reminder state) in your browser. After at least 7 days and 10 exports it may show one small card in its own popup asking you to rate it; it shows this at most twice, never on websites, and never sends the counters anywhere.

Optional page controls

If you press "Buttons under replies", ChatExport adds small Copy and .md buttons under the assistant replies on that page until you reload it. They only act when you click them.

What we do not do

We do not sell or share your data, use it for advertising or credit decisions, or transfer it to anyone. ChatExport does not load or run remote code.

Permissions

  • activeTab: read the conversation in the tab you open ChatExport on, after you click the icon.

  • scripting: run ChatExport's own reader on that tab, on demand.

  • storage: save your settings and the local reminder counters.
