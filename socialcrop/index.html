SocialCrop — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-09 (version 0.1.0)

SocialCrop is a Chrome extension that resizes and crops a picture you choose, drop or paste into it, to common sizes for social media posts.

What SocialCrop reads

Only the picture file you choose, drop or paste into its own popup. The picture is read inside the popup and drawn on a canvas on your computer. It does not read web pages, your tabs, forms, passwords or cookies, and it has no script that runs on websites.

What is stored, and where

Only the following, saved in your browser (chrome.storage.local) on your device:

  • your last choices: size, Fill or Fit, file type, quality, crop position and background colour. The picture itself is NOT saved: closing the popup forgets it;

  • three counters for one rating reminder: when it was installed, how many times you downloaded a picture, and how many times the reminder was shown and when, and whether you dismissed it for good. Removing the extension deletes all of this. The Clear button removes the picture from the popup.

What is sent over the network

Nothing. SocialCrop makes no network requests. Resizing, cropping and saving are done on your computer by code inside the extension. It has no server, no account system, no analytics and no advertising. We do not receive your pictures or any other data.

Uninstall page

When you remove SocialCrop, Chrome opens a page on our policy site (startuptalkyindia.github.io). The address contains only the extension name, its version and its ID. The page sends nothing; it shows a short "what went wrong?" form that opens an email draft you may choose to send. The site host may keep ordinary access logs.

Rating reminder

After at least 7 days and 10 downloads, the toolbar popup (never a website) shows one small card asking you to rate the extension. It is shown at most twice, can be dismissed for good, offers no reward, and the counters are never sent anywhere.

What we do not do

We do not collect, sell or share your data, use it for advertising, use it to judge creditworthiness, or transfer it to anyone. SocialCrop does not load or run remote code.

Permissions

  • storage: to save your last choices and the reminder counters on your device. It asks for no access to websites, no tabs permission, and has no always-on script.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the new text is published at the same address.
