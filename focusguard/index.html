FocusGuard — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-19 (version 0.2.1)

FocusGuard is a Chrome extension that shows how much time you spend on each website and helps you keep it in check with daily limits, pause-first sites and focus sessions.

What FocusGuard looks at

To do its job, FocusGuard checks which website is in the tab you are currently looking at, using the tab's web address. It does not read the contents of any page, what you type, your searches, or your other tabs, and it has no content scripts.

What is stored, and where

Everything is saved in your own browser (chrome.storage.local) on your device:

  • Your settings: limits, pause-first sites, focus lists, default focus length, strict mode.

  • Daily totals: for each day, the site name (for example "example.com") and the seconds you were active on it, kept for 60 days. FocusGuard never stores full page addresses, page titles or page content.

  • Hour-by-hour totals: for each day, 24 numbers of seconds, kept for 60 days.

  • Your focus forest: the date, length and result (grown or withered) of each focus session, and temporary "extra time" passes.

Time is counted only while you are active; time while you are idle or your screen is locked is not counted.

What is sent over the network

Nothing. FocusGuard has no server, no account system, no analytics, no advertising and makes no network requests. We do not receive any of your data.

Uninstall page

When you remove FocusGuard, Chrome opens a page on our policy site (startuptalkyindia.github.io). The address contains only the extension name, its version and its ID. The page sends nothing: it shows a short "what went wrong?" form that opens an email draft you may choose to send. The site host may keep ordinary web-server access logs.

Rating reminder

After you have had FocusGuard for at least 7 days and used it at least 10 times, the popup may show one small card asking if you would like to rate it. It uses local counters in your browser (install date, a use count, and whether you dismissed it), shown to everyone in the same way, at most twice, and never sent anywhere. "Don't ask again" stops it for good. It appears only inside FocusGuard's own popup, never on websites.

Your control

  • "Download CSV" (Stats page or Settings) saves your daily totals to a file on your device. It is created in your browser and never uploaded.

  • "Delete all tracked history" in Settings removes the daily and hour-by-hour totals.

  • Removing the extension removes all of its data.

What we do not do

We do not sell or share your data, use it for advertising, use it to judge creditworthiness, or transfer it to anyone. FocusGuard does not load or run remote code.

Permissions

tabs (to see which site is in front and to apply your rules to that tab), storage (your settings and totals, on your device), alarms (a 30-second timer so time is counted and sessions end on time), idle (so away time is not counted). No website access permissions are requested.
