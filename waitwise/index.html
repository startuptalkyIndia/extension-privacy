WaitWise — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-19 (version 0.2.2)

WaitWise is a Chrome extension that shows one small card while an AI chatbot (ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini) is writing a reply. The card is either a "helpful moment" (a breathing prompt, an eye-rest or stretch nudge, a tip, a quote or one of your own reminders) or a clearly labelled "Ad from the developer" (a house ad) for the developer's own products. You can choose "Helpful moments only" and never see an ad.

What WaitWise reads

On chatgpt.com, claude.ai and gemini.google.com, WaitWise checks only whether the site's "Stop" button is on the page, to know when a reply is being written. It does not read, copy, store or transmit your prompts, the chatbot's replies, or any other page content.

What is stored, and where

Everything is saved in your browser's local extension storage, on your own device:

  • Your settings: on/off switches (overall and per site), quiet hours, snooze, card position, the daily limit on ads.

  • Your own reminder lines, if you add any.

  • Counters: how many lines were shown today and in total, and how many replies a card was shown for (used only for the rating reminder below), plus the date you installed.

  • Waiting-time totals: for each day, an estimate in seconds of the time a reply was being written, kept for 14 days, used for the "time spent waiting" insight. You can switch this off.

The popup can show you everything WaitWise has stored. Nothing is stored anywhere else.

What is sent over the network

Nothing. WaitWise has no server, no account, no analytics and no tracking, and makes no network requests. Lines and tips come from lists built into the extension. If you click an ad, the link opens in a new tab and that website's own policy applies.

Advertising

The ads are house ads for the developer's own products. There are no third-party advertisers and no ad networks, and WaitWise does not pay you or anyone else.

What we do not do

We do not sell or share your data, use it for advertising, use it to judge creditworthiness, or transfer it to anyone. WaitWise does not load or run remote code.

Uninstall page

When you remove WaitWise, Chrome opens a page on our policy site (startuptalkyindia.github.io). The address contains only the extension name, version and extension ID. Nothing is sent from that page: it shows an optional "what went wrong?" form that opens an email draft which you may send yourself. The site host may keep ordinary web access logs.

Rating reminder

After at least 7 days and 10 replies with a card shown, the toolbar popup (never a website, never a notification) may show a small card asking for a rating on the Chrome Web Store. It is shown the same way to everyone, offers nothing in return, and can be dismissed: "Not now" lets it ask at most once more after 30 days, and "Don't ask again" stops it. It is shown at most twice, counted from the moment it appears, whether or not you react to it. The counters behind it are stored only on your device and never sent anywhere.

Your control

Switch WaitWise off, or off per site, from the toolbar popup; snooze it for an hour; turn off the waiting-time counter; dismiss any card with its x; or remove the extension, which deletes everything it stored.

Permissions

storage (your settings, reminders and counters, on your device) and access to chatgpt.com, claude.ai and gemini.google.com (to check for the Stop button and draw the card on those pages). No other permissions.
