WaitWise — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-19

WaitWise is a Chrome extension that shows one small, clearly labelled line from the developer's own products while an AI chatbot (ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini) is writing a reply.

What WaitWise reads

On chatgpt.com, claude.ai and gemini.google.com, WaitWise checks only whether the site's "Stop" button is on the page, to know when a reply is being written. It does not read, copy, store or transmit your prompts, the chatbot's replies, or any other page content.

What is stored, and where

Only your settings (on/off switches, daily limit) and two counters (lines shown today and in total), saved in your browser's local extension storage on your own device. Nothing is stored anywhere else.

What is sent over the network

Nothing. WaitWise has no server, no account, no analytics and no tracking, and makes no network requests. The lines it shows come from a list built into the extension. If you click a line, the link opens in a new tab and that website's own policy applies.

Advertising

The lines are house ads for the developer's own products. There are no third-party advertisers and no ad networks.

Your control

Switch WaitWise off, or off per site, from the toolbar popup; dismiss any line with its x; or remove the extension, which deletes the stored settings and counters.
