PostCraft — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-07 (version 0.1.0)

PostCraft is a Chrome extension that checks the length of a social media post you type or paste, shows where platforms cut it off, and splits long text into numbered posts.

What PostCraft reads

Only the text you type or paste into its own popup. It does not read web pages, your tabs, forms, passwords, cookies or anything on websites, and it has no script that runs on websites.

What is stored, and where

Only the following, saved in your browser (chrome.storage.local) on your device:

  • your draft text (so closing the popup does not lose it), the platform you picked and the numbering style you chose;

  • three counters for one rating reminder: when it was installed, how many times you copied text, and how many times the reminder was shown and when, and whether you dismissed it for good. Removing the extension deletes all of this. The Clear button in the popup deletes the saved draft at any time.

What is sent over the network

Nothing. PostCraft makes no network requests. It has no server, no account system, no analytics and no advertising. We do not receive your text or any other data.

Uninstall page

When you remove PostCraft, Chrome opens a page on our policy site (startuptalkyindia.github.io). The address contains only the extension name, its version and its ID. The page sends nothing; it shows a short "what went wrong?" form that opens an email draft you may choose to send. The site host may keep ordinary access logs.

Rating reminder

After at least 7 days and 10 copies, the toolbar popup (never a website) shows one small card asking you to rate the extension. It is shown at most twice, can be dismissed for good, offers no reward, and the counters are never sent anywhere.

What we do not do

We do not collect, sell or share your data, use it for advertising, use it to judge creditworthiness, or transfer it to anyone. PostCraft does not load or run remote code and does not post anything on your behalf.

Permissions

  • storage: to save your draft, your choices and the reminder counters on your device. It asks for no access to websites, no tabs permission, and has no always-on script.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the new text is published at the same address.
