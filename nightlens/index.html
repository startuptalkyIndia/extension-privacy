NightLens — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-19 (version 0.2.1)

NightLens is a Chrome extension that makes web pages dark so they are easier to read.

What NightLens reads

NightLens runs on the web pages you open (http and https). To decide whether a page is already dark, it reads the page's background colours, on your device. It does not read the text, forms, links, cookies or images on the page, and it does not record which pages you visit.

What is stored, and where

Your settings: whether dark mode is on, the mode (always, system theme or night hours), the schedule times, brightness and contrast, and the list of websites (by name, for example "example.com") you chose to exclude, force dark, or show with inverted pictures. They are saved in your own browser (chrome.storage.local) on your device. They are not sent anywhere.

What is sent over the network

Nothing. NightLens has no server, no account system, no analytics and no advertising, and it makes no network requests. We do not receive any of your data.

What we do not do

We do not sell or share your data, use it for advertising, use it to judge creditworthiness, or transfer it to anyone. NightLens does not load or run remote code.

Uninstall page

When you remove NightLens, Chrome opens a page on our policy site (startuptalkyindia.github.io). The address contains only the extension name, its version and its ID. Nothing is sent from that page; if you choose to give feedback it opens an email draft that you can send or discard. The site host may keep ordinary access logs.

Rating reminder

After you have used NightLens for at least 7 days and with dark mode on at least 10 times, the popup may show one small card asking if you would rate it. It is shown the same way to everyone, at most twice (a second time only if you chose "Not now", 30 days later), and never again after "Don't ask again". It only appears inside the popup, never on websites. The counters (install date, number of uses, reminder state) are stored locally and are never sent anywhere.

Removing your data

Removing the extension deletes its settings from your browser.

Permissions

storage (to remember your settings) and permission to run on web pages (http and https) so it can darken them. No other permissions are requested.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the new text is published at the same address.
