GEO Analyzer — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-19 (version 2.1.1)

GEO Analyzer is a Chrome extension that audits a web page for how well AI search tools can find, understand and quote it.

What GEO Analyzer reads

When you click "Analyze page", it reads the text and markup of the page in your active tab to run its checks. In the Tools page, it opens the pages you type in (Compare, Batch) in background tabs, reads them the same way, and closes the tabs. Nothing is read until you ask.

What is stored, and where

  • The score history: for each page you analyze, the date, score, grade and the pass/fail status of each check, for the last 20 scans (up to 100 pages). The page address is the key.

  • The last report, so that the print view can show it. These are saved in your own browser (chrome.storage.local) on your device. You can clear a page's history or all history in the popup, or remove everything by removing the extension. The extension does not store the full text of the pages it reads. The report keeps short excerpts of the most quotable passages and weakest section headings of the page you analyzed.

What is sent over the network

GEO Analyzer has no server, no account system, no analytics and no advertising. It does not send any data to us or to any third party. To check AI crawler access, it requests the audited site's own robots.txt, llms.txt, llms-full.txt and sitemap files, and one copy of the audited page, from that site only. These requests are made without cookies, the way a crawler would, and go only to the address you are auditing. The Schema helper and exports run locally. Exported files are saved to your device only.

Uninstall page

When you remove the extension, Chrome opens a page on our policy site (startuptalkyindia.github.io). The address contains only the extension name, version and ID. Nothing is sent from the page; the website host may keep ordinary access logs.

Rating reminder

The popup keeps local counters (install date, number of completed audits, whether you dismissed the reminder). After 7 days and 10 audits it may show one small card asking for a rating, at most twice, with a Don't ask again button. These counters are never sent anywhere.

What we do not do

We do not sell or share your data, use it for advertising, use it to judge creditworthiness, or transfer it to anyone. GEO Analyzer does not load or run remote code.

Permissions

activeTab and scripting (read the page you choose to analyze), storage (local score history), downloads (save the exports you ask for), and access to all sites (to fetch robots.txt, sitemap and llms.txt from the site being audited, and to audit URLs you enter in Tools). No page is touched until you click.

Changes
