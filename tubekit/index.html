TubeKit — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-09 (version 0.1.0)

TubeKit is a Chrome extension that helps you check YouTube chapter timestamps and a video's title, description and tags, from text you type or paste into it.

What TubeKit reads

Only the text you type or paste into its own popup (chapter lists, titles, descriptions, tags). It does not read web pages, YouTube or any other site, your tabs, forms, passwords or cookies, and it has no script that runs on websites.

What is stored, and where

Only the following, saved in your browser (chrome.storage.local) on your device:

  • what you typed or pasted and your choices (so closing the popup does not lose them);

  • three counters for one rating reminder: when it was installed, how many times you copied a result, and how many times the reminder was shown and when, and whether you dismissed it for good. Removing the extension deletes all of this. The Clear buttons remove the saved text and choices at any time.

What is sent over the network

Nothing. TubeKit makes no network requests. Checking is done on your computer by code inside the extension. It has no server, no account system, no analytics and no advertising. We do not receive your text or any other data.

Uninstall page

When you remove TubeKit, Chrome opens a page on our policy site (startuptalkyindia.github.io). The address contains only the extension name, its version and its ID. The page sends nothing; it shows a short "what went wrong?" form that opens an email draft you may choose to send. The site host may keep ordinary access logs.

Rating reminder

After at least 7 days and 10 copies, the toolbar popup (never a website) shows one small card asking you to rate the extension. It is shown at most twice, can be dismissed for good, offers no reward, and the counters are never sent anywhere.

What we do not do

We do not collect, sell or share your data, use it for advertising, use it to judge creditworthiness, or transfer it to anyone. TubeKit does not load or run remote code. TubeKit is not made by or connected to YouTube or Google.

Permissions

  • storage: to save your text, your choices and the reminder counters on your device. It asks for no access to websites, no tabs permission, and has no always-on script.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the new text is published at the same address.
