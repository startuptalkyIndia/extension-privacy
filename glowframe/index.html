GlowFrame — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-19

GlowFrame is a Chrome extension that draws a soft, blurred colour glow behind the video player on YouTube watch pages.

What GlowFrame reads

GlowFrame runs only on https://www.youtube.com/ and acts only on video watch pages. To make the glow it copies each video frame, at a tiny size, into a small drawing area inside the page, and takes a quick look at its colours and whether the picture has black bars or is still. It does not read the text of pages, comments, search queries, account details, cookies or anything you type. The copied pictures are not saved and are not sent anywhere.

What is stored, and where

Only your own settings, saved in your browser (chrome.storage.local) on your device: on/off, the look (strength, blur and spread, colour boost, brightness, contrast, edge fade, smoothing), updates per second, "always" or "theater only", and the switches for Battery saver, Auto quality, Smart rest and Ignore black bars. If you tick "Remember settings for this channel", GlowFrame also saves that channel's short handle (for example @name, taken from the watch page) next to the settings you chose for it, for up to 50 channels. Removing the extension deletes all of this. Export copies your settings as text to your clipboard only when you click Export; Import reads only the text you paste.

To adapt to your computer, GlowFrame reads the battery charge level and whether the computer is plugged in, and the number of processor cores and amount of memory the browser reports. These are used on the spot to choose a lower update rate and are not saved or sent anywhere.

Uninstall page

When you remove GlowFrame, Chrome opens a page on our policy site (startuptalkyindia.github.io). The address contains only the extension name, its version and its ID. Nothing is sent from that page; it only offers a form that opens an email draft you can choose to send. The site host may keep ordinary access logs.

Rating reminder

GlowFrame keeps three local things to decide whether to show one small rating card in its popup: the install date, how many videos you watched with the glow, and whether you dismissed the card. They stay on your device, are never sent anywhere, and the card is shown at most twice and never on websites. It is shown to everyone alike and offers nothing in return for a rating.

What is sent over the network

Nothing. GlowFrame makes no network requests. It has no server, no account system, no analytics and no advertising. We do not receive any of your data.

What we do not do

We do not collect, sell or share your data, use it for advertising, use it to judge creditworthiness, or transfer it to anyone. GlowFrame does not load or run remote code.

Permissions

  • storage: to save your settings.

  • Access to https://www.youtube.com/*: so the glow can appear behind the player on watch pages.

Not affiliated

GlowFrame is an independent extension and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by YouTube or Google.
