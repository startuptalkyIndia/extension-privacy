MailHTML - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-24 (version 0.1.1)

MailHTML is a Chrome extension that saves the email you have open in Gmail or Outlook on the web as an HTML file (or PDF, or a copy on your clipboard).

Contact: extensionsforseo@gmail.com

What MailHTML reads

Only when you click the MailHTML icon on a Gmail or Outlook web tab, it reads the one email message shown on that page: the message body, the subject line and the sender's display name (the last two are used only for the file name and the page title). It does not read other messages, your mailbox list, your contacts, other tabs or your browsing history, and it has no always-on scripts.

What is collected and sent

Nothing is collected and nothing is sent to us. MailHTML has no server, no account system, no analytics and no advertising. The email is cleaned inside your browser and the file is created on your own device. Two things you can switch on cause your browser to contact the servers that host the email's pictures, exactly as when the email itself is displayed: "Embed pictures" (the page fetches the pictures, without sending any cookies, so they can be stored in the file; a picture that would need your login stays a link) and "Load remote pictures" in the preview or PDF page. Those requests go to the senders' image servers, never to us. With "Strip tracking" on, tracking pictures are removed before any picture is fetched.

What is stored, and where

  • Options (file name template, cleaning choices) are kept in your browser (chrome.storage.local) on your device.

  • When you press Preview or Save as PDF, a copy of the finished file is placed in your browser's own database (IndexedDB) so the new tab can show it. Once that tab has shown it and stored its own reload copy in the tab's session storage (gone when the tab closes), the database copy is deleted. If the browser refuses the session copy because the file is large, the database copy stays so reloading works; anything older than one hour is deleted the next time MailHTML hands over a file.

  • Otherwise the email is held in memory only until the file is written.

Uninstall page

When you remove MailHTML, Chrome opens a page on our policy site (startuptalkyindia.github.io). The address contains only the extension name, its version and its ID. Nothing is sent from that page; it offers a form that opens an email draft which you may choose to send. The site host may keep ordinary access logs.

Rating reminder

MailHTML keeps local counters (install date, number of saves, reminder state) in your browser. After at least 7 days and 10 saves it may show one small card in its own popup asking you to rate it; it shows this at most twice, never on websites, and never sends the counters anywhere.

What we do not do

We do not sell or share your data, use it for advertising or credit decisions, or transfer it to anyone. MailHTML does not load or run remote code. Saved emails may be protected by copyright held by their senders; keeping them is your responsibility.

Permissions

  • activeTab: read the open email in the tab you click the icon on.

  • scripting: run MailHTML's own reader (and, if you choose Embed pictures, its picture fetcher) on that tab, on demand.

  • storage: save your options and the local reminder counters.
