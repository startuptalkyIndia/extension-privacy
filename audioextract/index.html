AudioExtract - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-24

AudioExtract is a Chrome extension that converts video and audio files you choose from your own computer into MP3, WAV or M4A audio files. It works entirely inside your browser.

What AudioExtract reads

Only the files you pick with the "Choose files" button or drop onto the converter page. They are read by your browser, decoded, cut, converted and offered back to you as downloads, all on your computer. AudioExtract has no access to websites, your browsing, your tabs, your history or any other file. It does not download anything from the web and does not accept web links.

What is stored, and where

Only your own settings, saved in your browser (chrome.storage.local) on your device: the output format, quality, channels, sample rate, volume option, fade lengths and file-name template. The converted files are held in memory for the time the page is open so you can download them; they are not saved by the extension and disappear when you close the page. Removing the extension deletes everything it stored.

What is sent over the network

Nothing. AudioExtract makes no network requests. It has no server, no account system, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not receive your files, their names, their contents or any information about you.

Uninstall page

When you remove AudioExtract, Chrome opens a page on our policy site (startuptalkyindia.github.io). The address contains only the extension name, its version and its ID. Nothing is sent from that page; it only offers a form that opens an email draft you can choose to send. The site host may keep ordinary access logs.

Rating reminder

AudioExtract keeps three local things to decide whether to show one small rating card on its converter page: the install date, how many files you have converted, and whether you dismissed the card. They stay on your device and are never sent anywhere. The card is shown at most twice, never on websites, and only inside the converter page. It is shown to everyone alike and offers nothing in return for a rating.

What we do not do

We do not collect, sell or share your data, use it for advertising, use it to judge creditworthiness, or transfer it to anyone. AudioExtract does not load or run remote code.

Permissions

  • storage: to save your settings and the small local reminder record described above.

  • No access to any website, and no other permissions.

Open-source software

MP3 encoding uses lamejs, a JavaScript port of the LAME encoder, under the GNU Lesser General Public License (lame.sourceforge.net). It runs locally and is included unmodified in the extension.

Your files and copyright

Use AudioExtract only for files you own or have the right to use. It does not download media from the web.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the new text is published at the same address.
