VolumeBoost - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-19

VolumeBoost is a Chrome extension that makes the sound of the current tab louder or quieter.

What VolumeBoost does with your pages

When you click the toolbar icon or press a shortcut, VolumeBoost adds a volume control to the audio and video players of the tab you are on. It does not read, record, copy or send the audio, the page text, or anything you type.

What is stored, and where

Only in your own browser (chrome.storage.local):

  • your settings ("Protect my ears" on or off, "Remember level" on or off);

  • if you tick "Remember level for this site": the site name (for example example.com) and a percentage, for up to 200 sites. The current level of a tab lives in that tab's page memory and is gone when the page is reloaded or closed. You can delete everything by removing the extension.

What is sent over the network

Nothing. VolumeBoost has no server, no account, no analytics and no advertising, and makes no network requests of its own.

What we do not do

We do not sell or share data, use it for advertising or credit decisions, or transfer it to anyone. VolumeBoost does not load or run remote code.

Permissions

activeTab (act on the tab you chose, only when you click or press a shortcut), scripting (add the bundled volume control to that tab), storage (your settings). No access to all websites.

Changes
