VolumeBoost - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-24

VolumeBoost is a Chrome extension that makes the sound of the current tab louder or quieter.

What VolumeBoost does with your pages

When you click the toolbar icon or press a shortcut, VolumeBoost adds a volume control to the audio and video players of the tab you are on. It does not read, record, copy or send the audio, the page text, or anything you type.

What is stored, and where

Only in your own browser (chrome.storage.local):

  • a few local counters for the rating reminder (install day, use count, reminder state);

  • your settings ("Protect my ears" on or off, "Remember level" on or off);

  • if you tick "Remember level for this site": the site name (for example example.com) and a percentage, for up to 200 sites. The current level of a tab lives in that tab's page memory and is gone when the page is reloaded or closed. You can delete everything by removing the extension.

Tabs playing sound (optional)

The popup lists the other tabs that are making sound. Without any extra access it shows only numbers ("Tab 1 - playing"), with buttons to switch to the tab and to mute it. If you press "Show tabs playing sound", Chrome asks for the optional "tabs" permission; if you allow it, the popup shows each tab's title, site name and small site icon so you can recognise it. These are shown in the popup only; they are not stored beyond the open popup and are never sent anywhere. The small site icon is the icon the site itself provides for its tab, so showing it makes your browser request that icon image from the site you already have open. To change the volume of another tab, you press "Allow volume control" on that tab, and Chrome asks for access to that one site only. You can withdraw any of this at chrome://extensions. While the browser is open, VolumeBoost keeps the IDs of tabs whose volume you changed (browser session storage) so those tabs stay in the list; this is cleared when the browser closes.

Languages

The popup is available in English, Spanish, Hindi, French, German and Brazilian Portuguese, chosen from your browser language. This uses Chrome's built-in translation files shipped inside the extension; nothing is downloaded.

What is sent over the network

Nothing. VolumeBoost has no server, no account, no analytics and no advertising, and makes no network requests of its own.

Uninstall page

When you remove VolumeBoost, Chrome opens a page on our policy site (startuptalkyindia.github.io). The address contains only the extension name, version and ID. Nothing is sent from the page; it shows an optional form that opens an email draft which you may choose to send. The site's host may keep ordinary web access logs.

Rating reminder

VolumeBoost counts, only in your browser, the day it was installed and how many times you used the controls. After at least 7 days and 10 uses, the popup may show one small card asking you to rate the extension, at most twice in total (the second time 30 days after "Not now"), and never after "Don't ask again". These counters are never sent anywhere, and there is no reward for rating.

What we do not do

We do not sell or share data, use it for advertising or credit decisions, or transfer it to anyone. VolumeBoost does not load or run remote code.

Permissions

activeTab (act on the tab you chose, only when you click or press a shortcut), scripting (add the bundled volume control to that tab), storage (your settings). Optional, only if you press the matching buttons: tabs (tab titles and sites for the list) and access to individual sites you choose (to change the volume of those tabs). No access to all websites is requested at install.

Changes
