QuickShot — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-19

QuickShot is a Chrome extension that takes screenshots of the tab you are looking at: the visible area, the whole page, or an area you select.

What QuickShot does with your screenshots

When you press a capture button or shortcut, QuickShot takes the picture inside your browser and saves it to your computer's Downloads folder. If you tick "Also copy to clipboard", it also puts the image on your clipboard. Screenshots are not uploaded, sent to a server, or kept by QuickShot after the file is saved.

What is stored

Only your settings, saved in your browser with chrome.storage.local: image format, quality, whether to hide fixed bars, whether to copy to the clipboard, and the short status line ("Saved") shown in the popup. QuickShot has no account system, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What is sent over the network

Nothing. QuickShot makes no network requests and loads no remote code.

Page access

QuickShot only works on the tab you are using, and only after you click its icon or use its shortcut (Chrome's "activeTab" permission). It does not run on pages in the background and it does not read page text. For full-page capture it briefly scrolls the page and hides fixed or sticky bars (unless you turn that off in Settings), then puts everything back. For "Select area" it shows a selection box on the page, which disappears before the picture is taken.

What we do not do

We do not sell or share data, use it for advertising, or transfer it to anyone. We do not receive any of your data.

Your control

Remove all saved settings by removing the extension. Delete screenshots like any other file in your Downloads folder.
