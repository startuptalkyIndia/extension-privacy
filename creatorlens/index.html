CreatorLens — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-24

CreatorLens is a Chrome extension that shows quick creator statistics for the YouTube channel page you are viewing and includes a manual engagement calculator.

What CreatorLens reads

Only when you click the CreatorLens icon while a YouTube channel page is open, CreatorLens reads text that is already visible on that page: the channel name, the subscriber count, and for each video card that is loaded, its title, view count, relative upload time ("3 weeks ago") and length. It does not log in anywhere, does not read private data, and does not scroll, click or navigate for you. It does not run on any page until you click its icon.

What is stored, and where

Nothing is stored unless you press "Save to list". Then the channel name, its address (the channel page only, without any query or tab part) and the computed numbers (for example subscribers and average views, with one snapshot per day so you can see growth) are saved in your browser (chrome.storage.local) on your device, up to 50 channels. The last report you open with "One-page report" is also kept there so the report page can show it. Calculator inputs are not stored. You can remove one channel or clear the list from the Saved tab, or remove everything by removing the extension.

What is sent over the network

Nothing. CreatorLens has no server, no account system, no analytics and no advertising, and makes no network requests of its own. We do not receive any of your data. "Copy Markdown", "Download CSV" and the printable report all happen on your device.

Uninstall page

When you remove CreatorLens, Chrome opens a page on our policy site (startuptalkyindia.github.io). The address contains only the extension name, its version and its extension ID. Nothing is sent from that page; it shows a short form that opens an email draft which you may choose to send. The site host may keep ordinary web access logs.

Rating reminder

CreatorLens counts, on your device only, when it was installed, how many channel analyses you have run, and whether you dismissed the reminder. After at least 7 days and 10 analyses it shows one small card inside its popup (never on websites, never as a notification) asking if you would rate it. It is shown the same way to everyone, offers nothing in return, and appears at most twice in total: once, and one more time 30 days later if you did not answer or chose "Not now". It never appears again after you choose "Rate", "Send feedback" or "Don't ask again". These counters are never sent anywhere.

What we do not do

We do not sell or share your data, use it for advertising, use it to judge creditworthiness, or transfer it to anyone. CreatorLens does not load or run remote code. It makes no automated judgement about any person; the collaboration checklist only counts boxes you tick.

Permissions

activeTab (read the channel page you clicked the icon on), scripting (run the extension's own bundled reader on that tab), storage (your saved list). No host permissions.

Not affiliated

CreatorLens is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube or Google.
