EditAnywhere — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-19

EditAnywhere is a Chrome extension that lets you temporarily edit, delete, blur, black out or highlight parts of a web page on your own screen.

What EditAnywhere does with a page

When you click the toolbar icon on a tab, EditAnywhere runs its own bundled code in that tab. The code draws a small toolbar and changes the page you are looking at as you click: it turns on Chrome's built-in editing, removes elements, or adds a blur, black bar or colour to them. It does not read, copy or analyse the page's content for any other purpose. It does nothing on a tab until you click the icon on that tab.

What is stored

Nothing. EditAnywhere does not use cookies, local storage, IndexedDB or any other storage, and it has no settings to save. Your edits exist only in the open page and disappear when you reload or close it. "Reset page" simply reloads the page.

What is sent over the network

Nothing. EditAnywhere has no server, no account system, no analytics, no advertising and contains no network code. We do not receive any of your data, including the content of the pages you edit.

Uninstall page

When you remove EditAnywhere, Chrome opens a page on our policy site (startuptalkyindia.github.io). The address contains only the extension name, its version and its ID. The page has a short "what went wrong?" form that opens an email draft you may choose to send; nothing is sent from the page itself. The site host may keep ordinary web access logs.

Permissions

  • activeTab: lets EditAnywhere work on the current tab, only after you click its icon.

  • scripting: lets it run its own bundled files in that tab. No access to all websites is requested.

Responsible use

Edits change only what you see on your screen, not the real website. Please do not use EditAnywhere to pass off an edited page as genuine or to mislead anyone.

What we do not do

We do not sell or share your data, use it for advertising, use it to judge creditworthiness, or transfer it to anyone, because we do not collect any. EditAnywhere does not load or run remote code.

Changes
