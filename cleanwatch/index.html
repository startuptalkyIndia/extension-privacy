CleanWatch — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-24 (version 0.1.0)

CleanWatch is a Chrome extension that makes the YouTube website calmer by hiding parts of its pages and adding limits and schedules that you choose. It is not affiliated with YouTube or Google.

What CleanWatch reads

CleanWatch runs only on www.youtube.com. To do its job it looks at the structure of the page (to hide or rearrange parts of it), at the page address (to tell a video, a list or a Short apart, and to open a Short in the normal player if you ask it to), and, on lists of videos, at the channel name or channel link of each row so it can hide channels from your own list. It does not read your account, your comments, your watch history, passwords or anything you type on the site. It runs only in the main page, not in embedded frames, and never on other websites.

What is stored, and where

Only the following, saved in your browser (chrome.storage.local) on your device: the profile and switches you chose, your schedules, your list of hidden channels, your daily Shorts limit, today's Shorts count (the identifiers of the Shorts you opened today, kept only for the current day and reset the next day), the day you last used it, and the counters for the rating reminder described below. Removing the extension deletes all of it. If you export your settings, the file is saved on your computer by you; it contains your settings only, not your Shorts count.

What is sent over the network

Nothing. CleanWatch makes no network requests. It has no server, no account system, no analytics and no advertising. We do not receive any of your data.

Uninstall page

When you remove CleanWatch, Chrome opens a page on our policy site (startuptalkyindia.github.io). The address contains only the extension name, its version and its ID. The page sends nothing; it shows a short "what went wrong?" form that opens an email draft you may choose to send. The site host may keep ordinary access logs.

Rating reminder

CleanWatch keeps three local counters in your browser: when it was installed, on how many different days it was in use, and whether you dismissed the reminder. After at least 7 days and 10 days of use, the toolbar popup (never a website) shows one small card asking you to rate the extension. It is shown at most twice, can be dismissed for good, offers no reward, and the counters are never sent anywhere.

What we do not do

We do not collect, sell or share your data, use it for advertising, use it to judge creditworthiness, or transfer it to anyone. CleanWatch does not block, skip or change advertisements, and it does not load or run remote code.

Permissions

  • storage: to save your settings and counters on your device.

  • Access to https://www.youtube.com/*: so it can change how YouTube pages look and behave as you asked.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the new text is published at the same address.
